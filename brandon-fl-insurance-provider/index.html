How the Plan Lanes Split — HMO to EPO

  • HMO: leans hardest on referrals and a single network, the tightest routing of the four.
  • PPO: loosens both rules in exchange for a larger share of the bill and wider out-of-network access.
  • POS: lands in between, trading referral structure against network reach.
  • EPO: drops the referral while still holding care to its in-network roster short of an emergency.

None of those distinctions in an insurance plan feel urgent the afternoon you enroll.
