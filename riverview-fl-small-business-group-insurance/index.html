Understanding What Employer-Sponsored Coverage Actually Is

A group health plan is coverage the company sponsors and offers to its W-2 staff under one shared policy. You choose the plan, decide how much of the premium the business picks up, and enroll the people who qualify. Employees can usually bring a spouse or children aboard by paying that added premium out of their own check.

Group-level rating is the quiet advantage here: nobody on your team fills out a health questionnaire to get in, and whatever slice of the premium you cover lands them a better deal than they would find buying an individual plan on their own out on the open market.

Riverview Employer FAQs on Small-Group Coverage

How many employees does a Riverview business need to start a group plan? Florida small-group coverage generally opens to businesses with 1 to 50 full-time-equivalent employees, though each carrier sets its own participation and headcount minimums. A licensed independent advisor can confirm where your Riverview company lands and which carriers will actually write it.
As the owner, do I have to contribute toward my employees' premiums? Most group plans expect the employer to cover a minimum slice of the employee-only premium — often somewhere around half, though it shifts by carrier and plan. That contribution is usually deductible as a business expense, which takes some of the sting out of the number.
Can a solo-owner shop near Winthrop set up group coverage? Sometimes — an owner who is also a W-2 employee of the company can qualify, but it turns on your business structure, state rules, and the carrier's underwriting. It is worth confirming with a licensed advisor before you assume you are in or out.
Should I put my Riverview staff on a group plan or send them to the individual marketplace? A group plan is chosen by the employer and covers eligible staff under one negotiated policy, usually with an employer contribution that lowers what each worker pays; individual plans are bought one person at a time. For most teams the group route lands better value, though an ICHRA can flip that math for a very small crew.
What are the tax angles when a Riverview business offers group coverage? The employer's premium contributions are generally deductible as a business expense, and employees can often pay their share pre-tax through a Section 125 plan, trimming their taxable income. The exact benefit depends on your structure and setup, so it is worth a word with your accountant alongside a licensed advisor.
