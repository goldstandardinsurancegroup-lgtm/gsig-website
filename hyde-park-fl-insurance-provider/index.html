How the Plan Lanes Split, HMO Through EPO

  • HMO: leans hardest on referrals and a single network — the tightest routing of the four.
  • PPO: loosens both rules in exchange for a bigger share of the bill and wider out-of-network access.
  • POS: lands in the middle, trading referral structure against network reach.
  • EPO: drops the referral but still keeps care to its in-network roster short of an emergency.

None of those distinctions feel urgent the afternoon you enroll. All of them turn urgent the Saturday a child needs stitches near Platt Street and the coverage is suddenly being tested, or the January a maintenance drug jumps a tier without warning.
