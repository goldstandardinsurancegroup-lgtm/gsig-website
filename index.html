Common questions

The ones we hear most, answered plainly.

Do you work with clients outside of Florida?

Yes. Sofia is licensed in 32 states and works remotely with clients nationwide. Whether you're in Wisconsin, Texas or anywhere in between, she can help you compare plans available where you live.

What's the difference between an HMO, PPO, EPO and POS plan?

They differ in how you reach doctors and specialists. HMO plans use a primary care physician who coordinates care and gives referrals, usually at a lower premium. PPO plans let you see anyone, in or out of network, without referrals, at a higher premium. EPO plans sit in between — no referrals, but in-network only. POS plans blend the HMO structure with an out-of-network option.

When is open enrollment, and what if I miss it?

Open enrollment for ACA marketplace plans typically runs November 1 through January 15. Outside that window you can still enroll if you qualify for a Special Enrollment Period — losing coverage, moving, marriage, or a new baby all commonly qualify.

How do I get coverage if I'm self-employed?

During open enrollment, or a qualifying Special Enrollment Period, you can shop ACA marketplace plans, and depending on your income you may qualify for premium tax credits that bring the monthly cost down substantially. Sofia will tell you either way.

What should I look at beyond the monthly premium?

The premium is the starting point, not the answer. Look at the deductible, the out-of-pocket maximum, copays and coinsurance, whether your doctors are in network, and whether your prescriptions are on the formulary. A cheaper premium with the wrong network can cost far more over a year.

What happens if I lose my job, or COBRA runs out?

Both qualify you for a Special Enrollment Period, giving you 60 days to enroll in a marketplace plan. In many cases an ACA plan matches or beats what COBRA would have cost.

Does working with an agent cost me anything?

No. Agents are paid by the carriers, so the premium is the same whether you apply through Sofia or go direct. You get the comparison and the year-round support at no added cost.
